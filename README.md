# Bodega Bay & Tomales Bay UAV Explorer (2019–2026)

Interactive Web GIS explorer for the **UCSC GeoFly Lab** multi-year coastal eelgrass (*Zostera marina*) UAV archive across **Bodega Harbor** and **Tomales Bay** (California).

**Live Explorer:** https://yuhuawang1010.github.io/bodega-bay-uav/

## Dataset Coverage
* **7 Survey Sites**: Westside, Mason's Marina, Campbell Cove (Bodega Harbor); Lawson's Landing (new 2026), Nick's Cove, Blake's Landing, Millerton Point (Tomales Bay).
* **95 Georeferenced Layers (2019–2026)**:
  * Complete 2019–2026 annual RGB orthomosaics across all 6 core sites (including multi-date summer & winter 2025 flights and 2019 multi-altitude surveys).
  * 2023–2024 Multispectral products: **MicaSense 10-band Dual** & **DJI Mavic 3M (5-band: RGB + 4 MS)** with live custom R/G/B band combinations and raw-radiometry **NDVI, NDRE, and GNDVI**.
  * June 2025 **DJI Zenmuse L1 RTK LiDAR** flight tracks & sample nadir imagery (Millerton Point & Westside).

## Key Features
* Site quick-jump & chronological multi-date **Time-Lapse Player**
* **Split-Screen Swipe Compare** (compare any two years, seasons, or RGB vs. NDVI)
* Live **10-band / 5-band Multispectral Band Combiner**
* Distraction-free Dark/Light Gray Canvas & Satellite basemap switcher
